What is Wavebench?

Wavebench is an audio editor accessed through your web browser. Use Launch Workspace to open the editing page and explore the tools available there.

How do I open the browser audio editor?

Select any Launch Workspace button on this page. It takes you directly to the Wavebench editor in the same browser tab.

Where can I explore audio trimming and splitting?

Open the workspace to check its current tools for your trim audio or split audio task. Available controls, supported file formats and export options are determined by the editor itself.

Can I switch between light and dark mode?

Yes. Use the theme button at the top of this homepage. The page follows your system preference initially and remembers your choice when browser storage is available. This setting applies to the homepage.
